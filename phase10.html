SPARK Workshop 1

Phase 10 · Transfer to Existing Teaching

Think about something you already teach or already do with students. Do not design another SPARK scenario. Start with an existing lesson, activity, project, lab, career interaction, or other experience.

  1. What are students already doing?
  2. Where is the consequential judgment? Where do students decide, interpret, diagnose, recommend, predict, choose, or conclude something meaningful?
  3. What is uncertain? What is incomplete, ambiguous, competing, or not immediately obvious?
  4. What evidence would actually matter? What information could change or sharpen the judgment?
  5. What minimal support could help, and what could you realistically observe? Add the smallest useful support without doing the reasoning for students, then identify what you could see, hear, or collect.
