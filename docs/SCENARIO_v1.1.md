# SPARK Workshop 1 — Data Center Decision Scenario v1.1

This is the scenario content used by the web app. It is fictionalized as a growing Central Arkansas community but grounded in public Conway-area records.

## Decision
**Proceed / Proceed with conditions / Defer pending more information / Oppose**

## Scenario architecture
1. Starting record + individual first impression
2. Need-to-Know
3. Choose exactly two evidence packets
4. Review evidence
5. Preliminary individual choices + group decision distribution
6. Evolving information update
7. Perspective challenge
8. Final decision + enforceable conditions/monitoring + reconsideration
9. Educator reflection
10. RIE transfer

## Evidence packets
- A. Fiscal, jobs & community benefits
- B. Energy, grid & ratepayer exposure
- C. Water, cooling & wastewater
- D. Infrastructure, construction & public services
- E. Land use, noise, environment & site risk
- F. Community, transparency & stakeholder process
- G. Governance, enforceable conditions & long-term oversight

## Provenance policy
- **PUBLIC RECORD** — official city, utility, planning, ordinance or agreement material.
- **LATER REPORTING** — current reported update, not silently treated as contractual fact.
- **UNRESOLVED** — intentionally missing information that creates a legitimate Need-to-Know opportunity.
- **Participant outside claim** — remains unverified until sourced.

## Core source basis
- City of Conway April 1, 2025 special City Council agenda / Forgelight Ventures MOU.
- City of Conway Planning Commission records for 2025 rezoning/planning.
- City of Conway data-center noise ordinance.
- Conway Corporation water/wastewater information and data-center FAQ.
- City of Conway May 2026 public committee meeting notice.
- Later 2026 reporting is used only as an evolving-information layer where primary records do not yet contain the update.
